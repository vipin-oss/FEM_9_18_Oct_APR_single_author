\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

A conforming Bogner--Fox--Schmit discretization was used to study Bloch waves in two-dimensional Mindlin Form-II elasticity with an oriented ellipsoidal length tensor and independent gradient micro-inertia. Displacement and derivative degrees of freedom were given consistent Bloch phases.

\begin{itemize}
  \item In homogeneous Case~H, rotation and aspect ratio change the zone-edge frequencies. The resulting directional separations do not form a complete band gap.
  \item With the ellipse area held fixed, the separation varies non-monotonically. The largest value among the sampled designs is $\Delta_{GX}=0.0677$ at $\theta=0^\circ$, $\mathrm{AR}=7$.
  \item Tensor rotation shifts the acoustic steering lobes without appreciably changing their peak angular deviation on the wavenumber ring examined.
  \item The periodic epoxy--YBCO cell has a Bands~3--4 gap on the computed meshes, although its width has not converged. Gradient micro-inertia also gives a finite high-wavenumber phase-speed limit of $0.3162$ for the transverse branch.
\end{itemize}

The next numerical step is further mesh refinement of Case~C. Extending the model to three dimensions would require a suitable $C^1$ volumetric element.
