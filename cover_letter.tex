\noindent Dear Editor,

I respectfully submit the manuscript entitled \textbf{``Directional Wave Dispersion and Energy-Flow Steering in Two-Dimensional Strain-Gradient Elastic Solids''} for consideration as an Original Research Article in \textit{International Journal of Solids and Structures}.

\textbf{Scope and contribution.}
The manuscript concerns wave dispersion and energy-flow steering in two-dimensional Mindlin Form-II strain-gradient elasticity with rotated intrinsic length scales and gradient micro-inertia. A conforming Bloch finite-element implementation resolves homogeneous directional spectral separation and group-velocity steering. A periodic material-contrast example exhibits a complete gap on computed meshes; its lack of mesh convergence is expressly acknowledged. The emphasis is on the mechanics and interpretation of wave propagation, rather than a claim to a new general-purpose finite-element algorithm.

\textbf{Disclosure of related work.}
For full editorial transparency, a separate manuscript by me addressing static boundary-value formulations in strain-gradient finite elements (Ref.\ \texttt{FINEL-D-26-00692R1}) is currently under revision at \textit{Finite Elements in Analysis \& Design}. That static study does not address Bloch--Floquet elastodynamic dispersion, gradient micro-inertia, higher-order Poynting energy-flux wave steering, or periodic phononic band-gap analysis, and shares no figures, tables, or numerical datasets with the present submission.

\textbf{Submission declarations.}
I confirm that the manuscript is original. It will be submitted here only after all earlier submissions of this same work have been closed or formally withdrawn. A separate Supplementary Material file (\texttt{Supplementary\_Material.pdf}) is attached alongside the manuscript. Thank you very much for considering this manuscript for \textit{International Journal of Solids and Structures}.
